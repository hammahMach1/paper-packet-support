Support

Paper Packet is a hand-drawn courier runner about carrying a message across the internet. This page is the support home for the iOS release.

Contact

Email engineering@ouzatech.com with the subject “Paper Packet support”. Please include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • How do I jump or glide? Tap once to hop. Keep holding through the fall to glide. The on-screen DUCK button ducks on phones; the bottom area of the screen also ducks.
  • My progress is missing. Progress is saved locally on the device. Deleting the app removes it. There is no account or cloud sync.
  • How do I change the sound? Use the SOUND button on the title screen or open SETTINGS from the pause menu.
  • How do I replay a level? Tap any unlocked level on the title grid. Completed levels show their best style grade.
  • How do I stop the haptics? Use SETTINGS → HAPTICS. Haptics are only sent on supported devices.
  • I found a bug. Email us with a screenshot or recording and the level name. Please do not include personal information.
