Support

Paper Packet is a hand-drawn courier runner about carrying a message across the internet. This page is the support home for the iOS release.

Contact

Email engineering@ouzatech.com with the subject “Paper Packet support”. Please include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • How do I jump or glide? Tap once to hop. Keep holding through the fall to glide. The on-screen DUCK button ducks on phones; the bottom area of the screen also ducks.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device, and in your own iCloud account if you are signed in to iCloud, so it follows you to your other Apple devices and returns if you reinstall. SETTINGS shows whether iCloud sync is active. If you delete the app while signed out of iCloud, the local progress is removed.
  • Do I need Game Center? No. Achievements, perks and stars all work inside the game. If you are signed in to Game Center, achievements and scores are also shared there.
  • The game feels slow on my older iPhone or iPad. Turn on SETTINGS → REDUCE EFFECTS. It keeps the same worlds with fewer particles and lighter effects. It is on by default on older devices.
  • How do I change the sound? Open SETTINGS on the title screen and switch MUSIC or SOUND.
  • How do I replay a stop? Open the route map and tap any stop you have delivered. Each one shows its stars and best score.
  • How do I stop the haptics? Use SETTINGS → HAPTICS. Haptics are only sent on supported devices.
  • I found a bug. Email us with a screenshot or recording and the stop name. Please do not include personal information.

Privacy

Ouzatech collects no personal data, analytics, or advertising identifiers. iCloud sync and Game Center are optional Apple services. Read the full privacy policy.
