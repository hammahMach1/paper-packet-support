Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026. Paper Packet is a single-player game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Ouzatech does not collect personal information, device identifiers, analytics, usage data, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. There are no Ouzatech accounts, ads, third-party SDKs, or tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game stores your progress, stars, best scores, achievements, perks, stamps, settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial in a local save file. Deleting the app removes this local copy.

iCloud sync (Apple)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a copy of the same progress in your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so your progress follows you between your Apple devices and comes back if you reinstall. This copy is stored by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Ouzatech cannot see or access it. You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for the app in your device’s iCloud settings, or by signing out of iCloud.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple’s Game Center service. Depending on your Game Center settings, your Game Center nickname and scores can be visible to other players on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, and Ouzatech does not receive your Apple Account or contact details. Achievements also work inside the game without Game Center. You can sign out in Settings → Game Center.

Purchases

Paper Packet has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads. Apple processes App Store downloads and payments under Apple’s own privacy policy; we do not receive payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Support email

If you email engineering@ouzatech.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to answer your support request and do not sell or share it. Delete it at any time by asking us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s next release.
