Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Paper Packet is a single-player game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Paper Packet does not collect personal information, device identifiers, analytics, usage data, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, third-party SDKs, or tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game stores your progress, best scores, grades, achievements, audio/haptics settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial in a local save file. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases and services

Paper Packet has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or external services. Apple may process App Store downloads and payments under Apple’s own privacy policy; we do not receive payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Support email

If you email engineering@ouzatech.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to answer your support request and do not sell or share it. Delete it at any time by asking us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s next release.
